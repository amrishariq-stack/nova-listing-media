FAQ

The quick answers.

How fast is delivery?+
Standard is by 9 AM the next business day. Need it same-day? Add 12-hour rush at checkout.
What areas do you serve?+
Virginia, DC, and Maryland — including Fairfax, Loudoun, Arlington, Alexandria, and Prince William. Ask about travel for outlying areas.
Do I need to be there for the shoot?+
No. Just arrange access — a lockbox or someone to let us in — and we'll handle the rest.
How should the home be prepped?+
We send a simple prep checklist when you book — lights on, blinds open, clutter away. We'll do a quick tidy of hero areas on arrival.
Do you do video and social Reels?+
Yes — it's our specialty. Agent-branded Reels for Instagram and TikTok come standard in our Social and Showcase packages.
How do I pay?+
Pay on delivery by card. Your gallery unlocks for download once payment is complete.
What if I'm not happy with a shot?+
We'll reshoot it. Your listing has to look right — that's the guarantee.
